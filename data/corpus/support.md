# Brightline Support

## Channels and response times
Starter has email support only, with a response within 48 hours. Team has email and chat support, with a response within 8 business hours. Business has 24/7 phone support, with a response within 1 hour for critical issues.

## Languages
Support is offered in English, Hindi and Spanish.

## Status
Live incident updates are posted on the Brightline status page.
