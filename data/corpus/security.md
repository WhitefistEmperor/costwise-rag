# Brightline Security

## Encryption
Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3.

## Two-factor authentication
All plans support 2FA with authenticator apps (TOTP). Hardware security keys are supported on Team and Business only.

## Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Business plan only.

## Sessions and passwords
Sessions time out after 12 hours of inactivity. Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Brightline is audited for SOC 2 Type II every year.
