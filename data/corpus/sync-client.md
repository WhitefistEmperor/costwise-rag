# Brightline Sync Clients

## Desktop
The desktop client runs on Windows 10 or later, macOS 12 or later and Ubuntu 22.04 or later. The maximum file size for the desktop client is 50 GB.

## Behaviour
Selective sync lets you choose folders. Edit conflicts create a "conflicted copy" file. Sync pauses when the battery falls below 15%.

## Mobile
Mobile apps need iOS 16 or later, or Android 11 or later.
