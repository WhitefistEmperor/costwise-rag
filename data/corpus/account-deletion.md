# Brightline Account Deletion

## Deleting a workspace
Only the workspace owner can delete a workspace. Deletion starts a 14-day grace period during which the workspace can be restored.

## Purge
After the grace period ends, all data is permanently purged within 30 days.

## Export and cancellation
Before deleting, data can be exported as ZIP archives of up to 200 GB with the Export tool. Cancelling a subscription takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
