# Brightline Sharing

## Share links
Share links expire after 14 days by default and can be extended to at most 365 days. Each link allows 100 downloads by default.

## Password protection
Password-protected share links are available on Team and Business.

## Guests
Team workspaces can invite up to 25 guest collaborators. Business workspaces can invite unlimited guests. Starter workspaces cannot invite guests.
