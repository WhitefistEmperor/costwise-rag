# Brightline Data Residency

## Regions
Workspaces can be hosted in Mumbai, Frankfurt or Virginia. The region is chosen when the workspace is created.

## Changing region
Changing region needs a migration ticket and takes up to 72 hours. Business workspaces migrate for free. Team workspaces pay a one-time fee of $150. Starter workspaces cannot change region.

## Guarantees
File contents never leave the chosen region. Only encrypted metadata is replicated globally.
