# Brightline Refund Policy

## Refund windows
Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After the window closes no partial or prorated refund is given.

## Processing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to process. Add-on storage purchases are non-refundable.

## How to request
Request a refund from Billing, then Request refund, or write to the billing team from the account owner's address.
