# Brightline Backups

## Backup frequency
Starter workspaces are backed up daily at 02:00 UTC. Team workspaces are backed up every 6 hours. Business workspaces are backed up hourly.

## Retention
Backups are retained for 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Team and 365 days on Business.

## Version history
Starter keeps 10 versions per file, Team keeps 50 versions per file and Business keeps unlimited versions.

## Restores
Restores are started from the Restore Center. Restores larger than 50 GB are queued and can take up to 4 hours.
