# Brightline Onboarding and Migration

## Free trial
The Team plan has a 14-day free trial with no card required. Trial workspaces are capped at 50 GB.

## Importing data
The migration tool imports from Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive. Migration is free for the first 500 GB. Beyond that it costs $0.02 per GB.

## Onboarding help
Business customers get a dedicated onboarding manager.
