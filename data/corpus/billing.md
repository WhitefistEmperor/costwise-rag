# Brightline Billing

## Invoices and payment methods
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Brightline accepts cards, UPI (India only) and wire transfer for annual Business plans. Prices are charged in USD or INR.

## Tax
Indian customers are charged 18% GST on top of the plan price.

## Failed payments
A failed payment is retried 3 times over 9 days. If it still fails, the workspace becomes read-only. After 21 days in read-only mode the workspace is suspended.
