# Brightline Pricing

## Plans
Brightline has three plans. Starter costs $9 per month and includes 100 GB of storage and 1 seat. Team costs $49 per month and includes 1 TB of storage and 10 seats. Business costs $199 per month and includes 5 TB of storage and 50 seats.

## Annual billing
Paying annually gives a 20% discount on the plan price compared with paying monthly for twelve months.

## Add-ons
Extra storage costs $5 per 100 GB per month and is available on Team and Business only. Extra seats cost $6 per seat per month on Team and $4 per seat per month on Business. Starter cannot buy add-ons.
